Zhichen Zeng

I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Hanghang Tong. My research interests lie in LLM and agents, personalization, and geometric ML.

Before Ph.D., I obtained my M.S. in Computer Science degree from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and B.Eng. in Automation from Zhejiang University.

I’m currently seeking research-oriented full-time opportunities. Please feel free to reach out if my background aligns with your interests.
